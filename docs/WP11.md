# WP11 — Life Story and galleries

From [Update 1](updates/UPDATE-1_Build_Specification.pdf) (ADR-048).

## What was built (10–11 Nov 2026)

- **Three ways to see a person**: Life story (the default for people who have died), Gallery, and Facts and evidence (the default for living people, whose stories aren't told). The Lifeline is one click away.
- **Life story**, built only from the tree's own facts and family links, with no AI and no credits:
  - At a glance: born, died, work, partners and children.
  - Chapters in date order: early life, marriage and family, work and homes, later life. Each sentence comes from a recorded fact and can show its sources in one consistent source card, whatever site the record came from. Remembered, inferred or disputed facts say so.
  - Dates read as words ("about 1890", "on 14 March 1960", "between 1990 and 1992"); wording that can't be parsed is kept as written.
  - Living relatives are mentioned without names or dates ("had a child who may still be living").
  - "The world around them": wars, laws and census years during their life with their age at the time, kept apart and labelled as context that doesn't say they took part.
  - "What isn't known yet": missing birth, death, parents, occupation or addresses, linking to Where to look next.
- **Person gallery**: every picture tagged to them, by decade, each saying whether it shows them, is a document about them, or shows their world. Others in a group photo link to their own galleries. Editors can reorder, edit the caption, date and place, or say "Not them".
- **Family gallery** (`/projects/{id}/gallery`): everything, by decade. One group photo appears in each tagged person's gallery.
- **Sort your photos**: pictures in the Archive not yet in a gallery, with suggested people from the file and folder names (a first name, strengthened by the surname) and the year in the name or the camera's date, checked against when people were alive. Each suggestion gives its reason; nothing is tagged until someone chooses. A surname alone isn't enough, because a folder called "Smith family" would otherwise suggest every Smith.
- Merges and removals carry gallery tags, so undo restores them.

## Acceptance (Update 1)

| Done when | Status |
|---|---|
| A person with 10+ records reads as a clear life, not a list of documents | Built and tested with synthetic people. Needs a real tree to judge |
| Photos can be added, tagged and reordered in seconds | Built (sort panel, tags, ↑/↓ ordering) |
| One photo appears in each tagged person's gallery | Passes |
| Every snippet links to its source and evidence label | Passes: each sentence's sources and status |
| Users don't need to work out which site a record came from | One source card style for files, records and saved finds |
| A test user prefers it to the equivalent Ancestry page | Not tested: needs the owner's sister or another genealogist |
