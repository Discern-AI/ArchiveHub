# WP13 — Lineages and record ordering

From [Update 1](updates/UPDATE-1_Build_Specification.pdf) (ADR-048).

## What was built (11 Nov 2026)

- **Lineages** ("Lineages" in the top bar): who held a role at a place or organisation, one after another. A lineage is the claims with a role (licensee, landlord, proprietor, owner, tenant, occupier, vicar, rector, minister, headteacher, master, mayor) about one subject, so every holder keeps its evidence and nothing new is stored.
  - **Succession timeline**: each holder as a bar, with the years nobody is recorded for shown as "Not recorded" between the people they separate, never filled in.
  - **Recent holders hidden**: anyone holding the role in the last 92 years may still be alive (an adult then, born within the 110 years ArchiveHub treats as living), so their name is hidden and the page says why.
  - **Connections**: a holder whose name (same first name and surname, or matching initial) and dates (alive and adult then) fit someone who has died in one of the user's family trees is suggested as possibly the same person, with the reason. It's never linked automatically.
  - **The Pigeons**: its five checked licensees (1881 to 1940) show as a lineage, with gaps 1882–1890, 1892–1900, 1929–1930 and 1932–1939, and the 1940 licensee's name hidden.
- **Record ordering** ("Order records" on a person's Facts and evidence view): records that could be ordered for each event, each with who holds it, what it should show, every fee listed separately (and "ArchiveHub fee: none"), delivery time, rights, and the source of the prices with the date checked.
  - Offers checked from the General Register Office on 11 Nov 2026: a certificate (£12.50, fourth working day with an index reference), a PDF (£8.00, births 1837 to 100 years ago and deaths 1837–1957, index reference needed) and an online view of the image (£3.00, same years).
  - No offers outside England and Wales, before 1837 (parish registers instead), or for anyone who may be living.
  - **Referral only** for now: the person orders from the holder and the order is tracked here: not ordered yet, ordered (with the holder's reference), received (with the copy uploaded to the Archive), or cancelled. Steps out of order are refused.
